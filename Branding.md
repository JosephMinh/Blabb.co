# Blabb brand

## Colors

- Plum: `#170A1C`
- Aqua: `#88E0D9`
- Coral: `#EF8354`
- Lilac: `#EDDFEF`
- Forest: `#32533D`

## Typography

Nunito — variable weights from 200 through 900.

## Logo

The canonical transparent mark is stored at `assets/blabb-mark.png`. The original square lockup is stored at `assets/blabb-logo.png`.

Use the plum mark on aqua, lilac, or white backgrounds. Keep clear space around the mark equal to the diameter of either counter in the letter B.

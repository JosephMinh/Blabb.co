Good to know

A few quick answers.

Does Blabb need the internet?+

Only to download a speech model during setup. After that, dictation works in airplane mode.

Which phones are supported?+

The current testing build supports Android 13 or newer. Blabb checks your device during setup and selects a suitable local model.

Why does Blabb use AccessibilityService?+

It lets Blabb detect the active text field, place the bubble, and insert your words at the cursor. Blabb ignores password and PIN fields and does not keep field text or app history.

Is it really free?+

Yes. The current testing build is free, with no account, ads, or subscription.
